**A Novel Multi-Layered Realtime Approach to Ensure Distracted Young Driver's Safety using Deep Learning and Geolocation**

**Awarded: 3rd place Category Award for Software Engineering (from 100’s of entries)**

**Context:**
Driven by the alarming statistic that nine teens die every day in car crashes, with distracted driving being a leading cause, I developed an affordable AI-powered safety device to protect young drivers when expensive commercial solutions remain out of reach for most families. I have attached the research abstract for this project submitted to the Synopsys Science & Engineering Championship in 2023. The Synopsys Championship is one of the premier regional science and engineering fairs in the United States, run by the Santa Clara Valley & Engineering Fair Association. Each year, students present original research projects across 21 scientific disciplines, undergoing rigorous evaluation by PhD-level scientists, engineers, and industry professionals.

**Abstract**

According to the CDC, every day an average of 9 teens die in a car crash with hundreds more injured. The Transport Research Laboratory reported that in 2022, more than 3,500 teens died in distracted driving related car crashes. Currently, the only solution is an expensive safe for the mobile device that has to be professionally wired into the car. I have created an inexpensive AI powered device that can be easily mounted on the car to monitor the safety of a young driver. The device consists of a programmed Raspberry Pi connected to a series of components including camera, speaker, and a GPS Antenna. While the GPS Antenna analyzes the speed of the car, the camera in the device takes an image of the driver above the predetermined speed threshold. An AI Algorithm running in the cloud receives, analyzes and determines if the young driver is distracted, and returns the results back to the Pi. If the young driver is detected to be distracted, the Pi then plays an audio alert in the car and sends a text message to the parent of the young driver. A prototype of this device was built and tested. After field testing each component of the device, the entire device was tested as a whole. In 20 trials, the device performed correctly 90% of the time, thus establishing the efficacy of the device. Using such a device, thousands of teenage lives can be saved, while providing peace of mind to their parents.
